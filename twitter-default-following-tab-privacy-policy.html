Privacy Policy for Twitter Default Following Tab

Last Updated: December 22, 2025

Overview

Twitter Default Following Tab is a browser extension that automatically switches to the "Following" tab when you visit Twitter/X. This privacy policy explains how the extension works and what data it does and does not collect.

Data Collection

We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any user data whatsoever.

This extension:

  • ✅ Does NOT collect any personal information
  • ✅ Does NOT track your browsing activity
  • ✅ Does NOT store any data locally or remotely
  • ✅ Does NOT use cookies or analytics
  • ✅ Does NOT communicate with any external servers
  • ✅ Does NOT access your tweets, messages, or profile information
  • ✅ Does NOT monitor what you read, write, or interact with

How the Extension Works

The extension operates entirely locally in your browser and only performs the following actions:

  1. Detects when you visit Twitter/X homepage - Monitors the current page URL
  2. Identifies the tab navigation elements - Locates the "For You" and "Following" tabs in the page
  3. Clicks the "Following" tab - Simulates a click on the Following tab if it's not already active
  4. Manages visual transitions - Temporarily hides content during the tab switch to provide a seamless experience

All of these operations happen entirely within your browser. No data leaves your device.

Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions to function:

Host Permissions

  • https://twitter.com/*
  • https://x.com/*

Why: These permissions allow the extension to run on Twitter/X pages and interact with the page DOM to switch tabs. This is the sole and essential functionality of the extension.
